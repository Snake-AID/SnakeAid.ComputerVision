# Experiment tracking (ClearML)

ClearML acts as the MLOps backbone for this project, centralizing metrics, parameters, artifacts, and model registry entries.

![ClearML home](img/Techstack/ClearML/home.png)

Why it is used:

- Track experiments (metrics, params, system info) and compare runs.
- Version and register trained models alongside artifacts and logs.
- Keep reproducibility high without relying on ad hoc storage.

Role in the pipeline: experiment tracking, model registry, and artifact management.
